# Proof Locker

Every claim a post makes points at a row in this table. No row, no claim.

## Grades

| Grade | Meaning | What a post may do with it |
|---|---|---|
| **SHOWN** | You can put it on screen: a screenshot, a recording, a before/after, the actual object, a named person who agreed to be shown. | Promise it in the hook and show it in the post. |
| **STATED** | It is true and you can vouch for it, but there is nothing to show. | Say it in your own voice as your experience. Not as a headline number. |
| **UNVERIFIED** | You believe it, or read it somewhere, and have not checked. | Nothing, until it is checked and regraded. |

Anything with no receipt at all is an **opinion**. Opinions are welcome — they are framed as "I think / in my experience", never as fact.
